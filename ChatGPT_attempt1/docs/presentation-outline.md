2. **Starting prediction.** Explain in your own words why greater viscosity at fixed density spreads momentum faster. Distinguish resistance to relative motion from slow transmission of motion.
3. **Demo.** Show the initially stationary interior and the moving upper boundary. Demonstrate doubled density, doubled viscosity, equal kinematic viscosity, and doubled gap. Keep the common physical clock visible.
